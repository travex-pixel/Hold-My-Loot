## The one-liner (use everywhere)
Gary's asleep. Monsters want his loot. Build a crew of Australian misfits and keep the pile.

## Short description (about 150 characters)
A funny tower defence game where a sleeping hero's loot is the health bar. 28 levels, 13 crew, 19 bosses. Nan
fixes most things.

## Long description

Gary is the realm's last licensed hero, and he is asleep on a pile of loot. Monsters walk the road to steal it.
Your job: build a crew of local misfits along the road and keep the pile.

- **13 crew with their own personalities.** Dazza throws whatever's in the bin. Big Marg bonks with a frying pan.
  Nan snipes with knitting needles and is overpowered on purpose. Bev summons bin chickens. A wombat on fire.
  A cassowary that kicks.
- **28 levels across three chapters**, each with a twist: creeks, gates, fairy rings, swinging bridges, sinkholes,
  blackouts, gusts that blow seagulls out to sea.
- **19 bosses**, from Kevin (a rat, bit bigger) to Sir Pantsalot, who steals pants and gets stronger with every pair.
- **Monsters wear what they steal.** Tap or swipe dropped loot to grab it back before they reach it.
- **Gary's Dreams**, tower abilities, synergies, mastery paths, Hard and Nightmare tiers, modifiers, Heat.
- **The Long Nap**: endless waves on a weekly seed. **Weekly Raids**: three minutes, as much damage as you can.
- **Plays anywhere**: free in the browser, phone or desktop, no install, works offline once played.
  Progress saves on your device.
- **No ads, no pay-to-win.** Every note of music is synthesised in the browser.

## Controls
Tap a wooden pallet to build. Tap a crew member to upgrade. Tap or swipe loot to grab it.
Desktop: click, plus Space to start a wave, 1/2/3 for speed, M to mute, Escape for the menu.

## Age and content notes
Cartoon slapstick. Jokes about whisky and the pub (Simpsons-level). No blood, no gambling, no ads,
no purchases. No account, no personal data (see https://holdmyloot.com/privacy.html).

### itch.io

  `preview.jpg`. Tick "This file will be played in the browser".
- Embed: 400 x 780, "Mobile friendly", orientation portrait, "Fullscreen button".
- Classification: Games, Strategy. Pricing: No payments (or "pay what you want" with $0 minimum, if you want tips there
  too).
- Cover image: crop `landscape-1-big-wave.jpg` to 630x500. Screenshots: all six portrait shots.
