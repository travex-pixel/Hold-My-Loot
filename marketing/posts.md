# Posting copy for every video

Every link carries a ref code so PostHog shows which video and platform brought players who stayed
(see `strategy.md`, Measurement). Swap `x`/`yt`/`tt` for the platform you post on:
`?ref=x-ep01`, `?ref=yt-ep01`, `?ref=tt-ep01`.

General rules: post at 8 am your time on a weekday, reply to every comment for the first six hours, pin a comment
with the link, and never edit a caption after posting (it resets reach on some platforms).
