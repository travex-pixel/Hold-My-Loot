## 1. Reddit (r/WebGames first; a week later r/towerdefense and r/IndieGaming, with their own ref codes)

**Title:** I made a free tower defence game where the hero sleeps through the whole thing

**Body:**

Gary is the realm's last licensed hero. He's asleep on his loot pile, and the pile is your health bar. Monsters walk
the road to steal it and wear whatever they take. You build a crew of Australian misfits along the road to stop them.

A few things I'm proud of:

- Nan snipes with knitting needles and is deliberately overpowered. I nerfed her once. A real player complained.
  It's now a written rule that Nan stays broken.
- A king called Sir Pantsalot steals pants and gets stronger with every pair.
- The whole soundtrack is synthesised in the browser. There are zero audio files.
- It's one HTML file. Plays on a phone or a laptop, no install, works offline once you've played.

28 levels, 13 crew, 19 bosses, a weekly endless mode and a weekly raid boss. No ads, nothing to buy.

https://holdmyloot.com/?ref=reddit-launch

I'd love to know where you got stuck or bored. There's a "Tell the dev" button on the home screen, or just reply here.

## 3. itch.io devlog / Discord announcement

**Title:** Build 31: Hold My Loot is ready for visitors

Hold My Loot is a free tower defence game about a hero who never wakes up. Build 31 is the "make it ready for
strangers" release:

- A new player's first game is a one-minute coached taster that's very hard to lose.
- Every tap that can't happen tells you why; every loss tells you what to try next.
- 8,400 bot games across every level, difficulty and modifier found four unfair spots. All fixed, without touching
  Nan.
- It now works offline, saves three copies of your progress, and opens in about a second on a mid-range phone.
- A privacy page, and analytics that ask first and store nothing until you say yes.
