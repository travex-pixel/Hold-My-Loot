What real players did on build 30

PostHog, builds 29-30, 45 days to 11 November. An earlier version of this page said 106 new players and that nine in ten never won. That counted crawlers: link-preview bots load the page and fire the tutorial events without anyone touching it, and the game sent no user agent, so PostHog flagged everyone as a bot. Counting only people who tapped or stayed 30 seconds:

  • Loaded the game125
  • A real person (tapped or stayed 30 s)68
  • Placed a crew member57
  • Finished a wave35
  • Won a level17
  • Came back another day15

Of the real players who placed a crew member, 30% won a level. That is the number build 31 has to beat; the rule for judging it is written down in review/build-31-success-criteria.md: 13 wins from the first 20 real players proves an improvement (p < 0.01). Build 31 now sends the browser's user agent and a first_input event, so future numbers leave crawlers out. Level 1 was also the level real players lost most often (9 of 24 losses). The Laneways showed up too, matching the bots. The dashboard "Hold My Loot: launch" in PostHog splits this funnel by build, so build 31 can be compared directly.

Ready for CrazyGames

Real traffic is about 1.5 people a day, almost all on phones, and none of it from the videos yet. Game portals are where a free web game finds players, and they are mostly played on desktop in a landscape frame. Build 31 now fills that frame, and tools/build-portal.mjs makes a CrazyGames zip: about 340 KB, no links back to the website, no Ko-fi, fonts inside the zip. It was tested in a cross-origin iframe at 907×510, 1280×720 and 800×450 with every outside request blocked, and a mouse player won the coached first game there.

An independent review agent then played the build as a portal QA reviewer and failed it on four points. Play stats were sent before the player agreed. The Privacy link opened a playable copy of the game outside the portal. A new player who didn't click lost the first level, and "Try again" gave the harder full level. At 907×510 the main buttons fell below the fold. It also found the mouse wheel scrolling the portal page. All five are fixed and each is now a test (22 portal checks). Removing the wheel fix makes the portal page scroll 2,000 px in the test, so the check is doing real work.

On screen

Balance: four unfair spots, fixed around Nan

A human-style bot (not an optimiser) played every level 30 times per setting: with and without Nan, on Normal, Hard and Nightmare, and with each modifier. 8,400 games in all.

  • The LanewaysNormal, recommended crew
    40%→97%

    Rat Mums kept taking the one lane nobody guards. They now take the long road in wave 3; waves 2 and 3 are a little softer; 310 starting gold.

  • Cliff Top GustsNormal, no Nan
    10%→70%

    Every stolen item was a seagull. The gusts that define the level now push flyers back harder, and the goblin-to-seagull swap starts later with half the birds.

  • Rat KingdomNightmare (was a walkover)
    97%→63%

    Won on Hard without Nan every time. Monster health up 27% on this level only; Normal is unchanged.

  • Swing BridgeChaos modifier
    30%→73%

    Chaos waves did not grow with later levels, so the crew was short of gold when Trevor arrived. Chaos waves now scale with level size and chapter.

How it was checked

Every suite loads index.html exactly as it ships. node tests/run-all.mjs runs them in order.

  • syntax / smoke-simevery script parses, the sim runs headless
  • taster-simthe first game cannot really be lost
  • first-minutethe coached first minute, iPhone SE and 15
  • saves20 checks: old saves, damage, full disk, blocked storage
  • offline7 checks: offline launch, updates reach installed copies
  • consent15 checks: nothing stored before yes, nothing sent after no
  • leaderboard-sql27 checks on a real Postgres
  • board-client9 checks against a mocked Supabase
  • support / share / ogKo-fi unlock, share cards, link previews
  • polish19 checks: zoom, wide screens at every portal size, keys, mute
  • portal22 checks: the CrazyGames zip in a cross-origin iframe, outside requests blocked, portal QA findings
  • bugbash12 checks: double taps, multitouch, rotation, old WebView, 400 random taps
  • perf-startup / perf-playtime to playable, frame budget, memory, layout shift
  • balance-matrix8,400 bot games: levels × crew × tiers × modifiers

Still open, in order
