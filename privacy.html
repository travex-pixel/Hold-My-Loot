Privacy

Hold My Loot is a free browser game made by one person. Here is everything it keeps and sends.

The short version
  • No cookies. No ads. No accounts. No names, emails or phone numbers.
  • Your progress is saved on your own device and nowhere else.
  • Anonymous play stats are only kept if you say yes. Say no and nothing is sent.

Saved on your device

The game uses your browser's local storage (not cookies) so you don't lose your progress. It never leaves your device unless you copy a save code yourself.

WhatWhy
Your progress, settings and saved run (three copies, so a crash can't wipe them)So the game remembers you
A random anonymous id, only after you say yes to play statsSo the stats can tell one player's second day from a new player
A random leaderboard id, only after you post a scoreSo the leaderboard can stop spam

Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it.

Play stats (PostHog)

To find levels that are too hard and places people get stuck, the game can send anonymous events to PostHog (servers in the USA): things like "level 3 started", "wave 4 lost", the game's build number, your screen size and whether you're on a phone, tablet or computer.

  • Before you answer the "Help me fix the game?" question, events use a one-off id that lives only in memory and is gone when you close the tab. Nothing is stored on your device for stats.
  • If you say yes, a random anonymous id is stored so your sessions can be counted together.
  • If you say no, nothing is sent at all, and the id is deleted.
  • You can change your mind any time in Menu, Settings, "Share anonymous play stats".
  • If you type feedback into "Tell the dev", that text is sent too. Please don't put personal details in it.

No profile is built about you, nothing is sold, and stats are never used for ads. Like any website, PostHog's servers see your IP address when the game connects; the game asks PostHog not to build person profiles.

Leaderboard

If the weekly leaderboard is on and you post a score, it stores the nickname you choose, your score, your crew and the leaderboard id. Nicknames are filtered and anyone can see them, so don't use your real name.

Supporting the game (Ko-fi)

The Support button opens Ko-fi in a new tab. Anything you do there is between you and Ko-fi; the game never sees your payment details.

Kids

The game doesn't ask anyone for personal information, at any age.

Questions or deletion

Use "Tell the dev" on the game's home screen. To delete play stats tied to your anonymous id, turn stats off in Settings and say so in your message.
